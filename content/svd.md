I built an SVD class to make low-rank approximations of images. Lower ranks keep broad shapes; higher ranks bring back more detail.

The interactive preview above lets you try this with your own image. The [Python code and Streamlit app](https://github.com/AlexZhai21/Image-Approximation-using-SVD) are in the project repository.
